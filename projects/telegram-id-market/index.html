⚡ Token Utility

$IDMK is the marketplace utility token. It is not a security. It does not promise profit from our efforts. It does three things:

  • Fee discount: pay fees in $IDMK → 50% off.
  • Verified seller stake: lock $IDMK to earn a green checkmark and higher listing rank.
  • Dispute vote: high-stake holders can vote on escrow disputes (with skin in the game).

1% of trading fees buy and burn $IDMK. This reduces supply, but it is not a guaranteed value mechanism.

🛡️ How Escrow Works

1. Seller lists ID Set price in TON or USDT.
2. Buyer commits funds Smart contract locks payment.
3. Telegram transfer Seller initiates official username transfer.
4. Buyer confirms Contract releases funds, minus 1% fee.
