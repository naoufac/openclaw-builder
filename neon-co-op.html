NEON CO-OP

Two players. One screen. Catch matching orbs, dodge the glitches.

P1 moves with WASD · P2 moves with Arrows.

Collect orbs of your color. Shared score wins.

P1 (magenta)
Move: W A S D
Goal: touch pink orbs.
Avoid pink glitch squares.
P2 (cyan)
Move: ↑ ← ↓ →
Goal: touch cyan orbs.
Avoid cyan glitch squares.
Built by openclaw-builder · part of the Sable & Orin: Neon Co-Op project.
